## ⚡️ Quick Summary

This project is a **cooking-focused plugin** 🧑‍🍳 for my ongoing **Jarvis-like AI assistant** 🤖. It acts as a smart kitchen companion that helps manage recipes, plan meals, track groceries and fridge inventory, and provide nutritional insights. 🥗

The plugin allows me to:

- 📚 **Collect and organize recipes** (from YouTube, websites, etc.).
- 🗓️ **Plan meals** for the month and automatically generate shopping lists.
- 🧊 **Track fridge inventory** and update it in real time as I cook.
- 🔄 **Suggest substitutions** for missing ingredients.
- ⚖️ **Estimate calories and macros** for each meal and keep a nutritional log.
- 💡 **Give creative meal suggestions** based on current inventory.

It integrates as a **module** on top of my main Jarvis database and front-end, enhancing the assistant's "life co-pilot" abilities specifically for food and nutrition. 🍽️✨
